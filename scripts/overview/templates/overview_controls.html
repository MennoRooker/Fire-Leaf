POKéMON FIRERED+

This overview acts as a mastersheet for the entire game, as a sort of replacement for Bulbapedia. You can consult this document for wild encounters, trainer battles and item locations. Every battle in the game is referenced here as well as every section that has any encounters. The sections are ordered based on what you encounter first throughout a normal playthrough of the game. I wanted to have all relevant information that wasn't specifically a new feature or a change I made to the mons or moves in the same place, so you can keep it next to you while playing. There are some filters that you can use to show or hide parts of the overview that are (ir)relevant to you.

Rematches

Rematches work a little bit differently from rematches in vanilla Fire Red. Rematches cannot be repeated indefinitely, and the player has to take 1500 steps after beating a trainer to be able to fight them again. Different colored icons are displayed based on how tough the rematch is and at what stage the rematch is unlocked.

VS Seeker stages:

  • InitialAvailable after obtaining the VS Seeker.
  • CeladonAvailable after reaching Celadon City.
  • FuchsiaAvailable after reaching Fuchsia City.
  • ChampionAvailable after becoming Champion.
  • SapphireAvailable after delivering the Sapphire.

You can toggle on/off rematches in this overview by selecting "Show rematches" below.
